One line, at most 200 characters, that gets its author out of a pointless meeting. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`; a sealed scorer gates it on length, and an AI judge pairs excuses head to head.

The line:

> I'd love to join, but I just watched my calendar get a meeting about this meeting and I need to sit with that.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes `entry.txt` (110 characters) to `/output/`.
